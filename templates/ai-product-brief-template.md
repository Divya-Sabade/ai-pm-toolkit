# AI Product Brief (one page)

From the free course *AI Product Management Foundations* · Sticky Notes on AI

**Feature name:**
**Author / date:**

1. **Problem and users.** Who struggles with what today, and what's their current workaround?
2. **Why AI.** Which fit questions does it pass (messy input, "pretty good" is valuable, cheap to check, frequent, has a fallback)? What does a wrong answer cost?
3. **What good looks like.** Five real example inputs, what a good output includes, and what it must never do.
4. **Context.** What the model needs to see, where that data comes from, how fresh it is, who may see it.
5. **Approach.** Which rung (prompting, retrieval, workflow, fine-tuning, agent) and why. What eval result would make you move up a rung?
6. **Quality checks.** Your top failure types and a pass/fail check for each. Who owns the bar?
7. **Experience and trust.** How people see, check, edit and undo. How handoffs work (what happened / what's uncertain / what to decide).
8. **Risk.** Failure modes that apply, designed response for each, risk tier, who signs off.
9. **Cost and speed.** Budget per request, maximum wait.
10. **Launch.** Stages, metrics (usage, task success, trust, cost per successful task) and the pause rule.
