# One-page AI risk review

From the free course *AI Product Management Foundations* · Sticky Notes on AI

**Feature:** **Date:** **Owner:**

1. **What could go wrong?** Tick the failure modes that apply and describe each:
   - [ ] Confident errors (made-up facts, policies, numbers)
   - [ ] Prompt injection (outside text tricks the AI)
   - [ ] Data leakage (private or another user's data)
   - [ ] Unfair outcomes (worse results for some groups)
   - [ ] Harmful or off-brand content
   - [ ] Over-reliance (people stop checking)
   - [ ] Wrong actions (sends, deletes, pays, changes something)
2. **Who could be harmed, and how badly?**
3. **Risk tier:** Low / Medium / High, and why.
4. **Designed response for each failure:** prevent, detect, recover, or hand off.
5. **What we'll monitor after launch, and the result that pauses the rollout.**
6. **Who signs off?**

This is a product checklist, not legal advice. Your legal, privacy and security partners make the final call.
